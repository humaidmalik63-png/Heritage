What the numbers mean

Similarity
The local CLIP cosine of the nearest indexed image. Meaningless on its own once the open-set gate has run.
Confidence
The confidence of whichever model made the decision — the local catalogue for a match, the vision model for a global identification.
Open-set gate
A match is only accepted when the top hit is separated from the field by a calibrated margin. Otherwise the system declines rather than guessing.

Grounding

Local results carry the catalogue metadata and the evidence records held with them, each with its named source and URL. A global vision identification is the vision model's own assessment — it is labelled as such and is never presented with an external citation it does not have.
