Recognition Metrics & Methodology

CLIP Similarity
Cosine similarity (inner product) in 512-dimensional CLIP embedding space between the query and the nearest indexed catalogue photograph.
Competitor Margin
The similarity separation (calibrated threshold: Δ ≥ 0.10) between the top candidate site and the nearest competing monument class.
Open-Set Gate
A candidate match is accepted only when similarity meets the threshold (≥ 0.85) and the competitor margin exceeds 0.10, preventing false acceptance of uncatalogued sites.
External Identification
Secondary multimodal vision identification invoked strictly when local recognition rejects an uncatalogued or out-of-distribution image.

Grounding

Local results carry the catalogue metadata and the evidence records held with them, each with its named source and URL. An external identification is the model's own visual assessment — it is labelled as such and is never presented with an independent catalogue citation it does not possess.
